Privacy Policy

🔒 No Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information you enter remains on your device.

📱 No Advertising

This app contains no advertisements. It is completely free and ad-free.

Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information and what data practices we follow.

Congé Calculateur is a vacation calculator application designed to help Moroccan customs officers calculate their annual leave and work timing. We developed this app as a free tool for our colleagues.

Information Collection

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers
  • No location data: We do not track or collect your location
  • No usage analytics: We do not track how you use the app
  • No device information: We do not collect device IDs, IP addresses, or device specifications
  • No financial information: We do not collect payment or financial data

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Vacation calculations (start dates, number of days, calculation results)
  • Calculation history
  • App settings (annual leave days, work schedule preferences, language selection)

This data never leaves your device. It is stored using your device's local storage system and is only accessible to you. When you uninstall the app, all this data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

Public Holiday Data

The app fetches public holiday information from the Calendarific API to provide accurate Moroccan holiday dates. When making these requests:

  • We only request public holiday data for Morocco
  • No personal information is sent with these requests
  • No user data is transmitted
  • The API provider may log standard web request information (IP address, request time) as part of normal web operations

Data Usage

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not use your data for any purpose. The local data stored on your device is used solely to:

  • Provide vacation calculation services
  • Display your calculation history
  • Remember your app preferences

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not collect any data.

  • We do not sell data (we have no data to sell)
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • We do not use data for marketing purposes
  • We do not transfer data to external servers

Advertising

This app contains no advertisements. There are no ad networks, no tracking pixels, and no advertising-related data collection.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected, there are no special considerations needed for children's privacy.

Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Delete: You can delete calculation history using the app's reset feature
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data
  • No account required: You can use the app without creating any account

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data security is primarily managed by your device's operating system security features. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Using device lock screens and security features
  • Being cautious when sharing your device with others

Since we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically.
