# User Guide - Vacation Calculator

## 📱 Introduction

This application was designed specifically for Moroccan customs officers to facilitate the calculation of annual leave days.

## 🎯 How It Works

### Basic Rules
- You have **22 working days** of annual leave
- **Weekends** (Saturday and Sunday) are automatically added and don't count
- **Public holidays** during your vacation are also automatically added and don't count
- The application automatically calculates your return-to-work date

### Practical Example

**Scenario:** You request 5 days of leave starting Monday, January 8, 2024

**Calculation:**
1. **Working days requested:** 5 days (Mon 8, Tue 9, Wed 10, Thu 11, Fri 12)
2. **Weekend added:** Sat 13 and Sun 14 (2 days)
3. **Total:** 7 calendar days
4. **Return date:** Monday, January 15, 2024

**If a holiday falls on Wednesday, January 10:**
1. **Working days requested:** 5 days (Mon 8, Tue 9, Thu 11, Fri 12, Mon 15)
2. **Weekend added:** Sat 13 and Sun 14 (2 days)
3. **Holiday added:** Wed 10 (1 day)
4. **Total:** 8 calendar days
5. **Return date:** Tuesday, January 16, 2024

## 📖 Using the Application

### 1. Home Screen

The main screen displays:
- **Remaining days:** Number of available vacation days
- **Percentage used:** Visualization of your consumed leave
- **Statistics:** Used days and annual total

**Available actions:**
- 🧮 **Calculate Vacation:** Plan new vacations
- 📜 **History:** View your past and upcoming vacations
- 📅 **Holidays:** See the calendar of Moroccan holidays
- 🔄 **Reset Year:** Reset to zero for a new year
- ⚙️ **Settings:** Configure annual leave days and preferences
- 📋 **Work Schedule:** Calculate your work schedule

### 2. Calculate Vacation

**Steps:**

1. **Select start date**
   - Tap on the calendar
   - Choose the first day of your vacation
   - The date can be today or in the future

2. **Choose number of working days**
   - Use the + and - buttons to adjust
   - Indicate only working days (Mon-Fri)
   - The application verifies that you have enough days available

3. **Calculate**
   - Tap the "Calculate" button
   - The application processes your request taking into account weekends and holidays

### 3. Calculation Result

The result screen displays:

**Main information:**
- 📅 **Return to work date:** Exact day of your return
- 📊 **Complete details:**
  - Start date
  - Working days requested
  - Weekends included
  - Holidays included
  - Total duration in calendar days

**Holidays:**
- List of holidays that fall during your vacation
- Name in French and Arabic

**Actions:**
- ✅ **Confirm and Save:** Save this vacation to history
- ✏️ **Modify:** Go back to adjust dates

### 4. Vacation History

View all your vacations:

**Information displayed:**
- 🟢 **Ongoing:** Current vacation
- 🔵 **Upcoming:** Planned vacations
- ⚪ **Completed:** Past vacations

**For each vacation:**
- Number of working days
- Start and return dates
- Statistics (total, weekends, holidays)

**Actions:**
- 👁️ **Tap on a vacation:** View complete details
- 🗑️ **Delete:** Remove a vacation from history (restores days)

### 5. Holidays

Complete calendar of holidays in Morocco:

**National Holidays:**
- New Year (January 1)
- Independence Manifesto Day (January 11)
- Labor Day (May 1)
- Throne Day (July 30)
- Oued Ed-Dahab (August 14)
- Revolution of the King and the People (August 20)
- Youth Day (August 21)
- Green March (November 6)
- Independence Day (November 18)

**Religious Holidays:**
- Eid al-Fitr (2 days)
- Eid al-Adha (2 days)
- Islamic New Year
- Mawlid (Prophet's Birthday)

**Indicators:**
- 🟢 **Today:** Current holiday
- 🟠 **Soon:** Holiday in the next 30 days
- ⚪ **Past:** Past holiday

### 6. Work Schedule Calculator

Calculate your work schedule based on timing systems:

**Timing Systems:**
- **12/24:** Work 12 hours, rest 24 hours (cycle: Day → Night → Covered Day)
- **12/24, 12/48:** Day shift 12h rest 24h, Night shift 12h rest 48h (cycle: Day → Night → Covered Day → Compensatory Rest)

**Features:**
- Customizable start time (default: 09:00)
- Select current work type (Day, Night, Covered Day, Compensatory Rest)
- Check any date to see work type
- Visual calendar showing work schedule for entire month

## 💡 Usage Tips

### Optimal Planning

1. **Check holidays** before planning
2. **Take advantage of bridges:** A holiday in the middle of the week can be advantageous
3. **Check your remaining days** regularly

### Annual Management

1. **Start of year:** Use "Reset Year" to start fresh with 22 days
2. **Regular tracking:** Check history to see your past vacations
3. **Planning:** Keep some days for unexpected events

### Strategy Examples

**Example 1: Extended weekend**
- Request 1 day on Friday
- Result: 3 days off (Fri-Sat-Sun)
- Cost: 1 working day

**Example 2: Full week**
- Request 5 days from Monday to Friday
- Result: 9 days off with weekends
- Cost: 5 working days

**Example 3: With holiday**
- Holiday on Wednesday
- Request 2 days (Monday and Tuesday)
- Result: 5 days off (Mon-Tue-Wed-Sat-Sun)
- Cost: 2 working days

## ⚠️ Important Points

### Religious Holiday Dates
The dates of Islamic holidays are approximate and may vary by 1-2 days based on moon observation. Always verify with official announcements.

### Data Backup
- Data is automatically saved on your device
- If uninstalled, data will be lost
- Remember to note your important vacations

### Reset
- The "Reset Year" action is **irreversible**
- Use it only at the start of the calendar year
- All history will be erased

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Application doesn't calculate correctly
- Verify that you have enough days available
- Make sure the start date is valid
- Restart the application if necessary

### Holidays don't display
- Check your Internet connection (first use)
- The application uses backup data if necessary
- Religious holidays may require an update

### I can't confirm a vacation
- Verify that you have enough remaining days
- Make sure you've calculated the vacation before confirming
